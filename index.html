Data and methods

One observation is one sale, keyed by listing ID. City combines city and state. Campus area uses the university assigned to each sale. All amounts are nominal US dollars. The main sample includes prices from $10,000 through $5,000,000. Q2 and Q9 use all rows meeting their own criteria. Q3 compares both price scopes. A median averages the two middle values for an even sample. Ties use unrounded values.

Days on market measures calendar days from listing to contract. Both dates are required, and same-day contracts count as zero. Year and month come from the sale date. Basement blanks are unknown. Distance measures straight-line miles to the assigned campus landmark. The map locates each campus area at the median latitude and longitude of its in-scope sales. Q6 sums only positive taxes below 10% of the corresponding price. The first and last sale years are partial. These transactions describe the collection, not every local home or a constant-quality price index.

Source · University of Illinois–Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies, College-town home sales, version 1.0 (2026).
